page-1: Overview

Entry: initial route. Exit: row click to page-2 or primary action to page-3.
1[Header and Status]

Intent: user understands where they are and whether action is needed.

Why here: orient before showing work items.

2[Summary]

Intent: user sees count, urgency, and current state.

Why here: above-fold decision support.

3[Primary Action]

Intent: user knows the next main action.

Why here: isolated from secondary actions.

4[Work Area]

Intent: user can scan, select, and inspect items.

Why here: follows summary and action.
